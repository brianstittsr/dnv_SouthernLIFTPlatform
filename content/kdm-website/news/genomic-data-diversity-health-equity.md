## The Genomic Data Crisis: Understanding the Scale

### The Missing 88%

The HITLAB evidence paper reveals that current genomic biorepositories—spanning public, private, academic, and national systems—fail to represent the vast majority of human genetic diversity. This structural gap has profound implications:

- **Precision Medicine Inequality**: AI-enabled drug discovery and personalized treatments are being built on incomplete genetic foundations
- **BioDefense Vulnerabilities**: National security preparedness depends on representative genomic data for pathogen response
- **Research Bias**: Clinical trials and therapeutic development systematically exclude diverse populations
- **Federal Compliance**: The FDA and NIH now mandate diversity in research, creating urgent demand for representative biorepositories

### Regulatory Tailwinds

The federal government has recognized this crisis and is actively legislating solutions:

- **FDA Diversity Mandates**: New requirements for representative clinical trial participation
- **NIMHD Priorities**: The National Institute on Minority Health and Health Disparities has prioritized genomic research equity
- **ARPA-H Initiatives**: The Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health is funding breakthrough approaches to inclusive biomedical research
- **DoD BioDefense**: The Department of Defense requires representative genomic data for national security applications
