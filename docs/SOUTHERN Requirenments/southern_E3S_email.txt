From the E3S\Nimble perspective, the opportunity is to commercialize the Cognitive Digital Twin (CDT) through software licensing, strategic partnerships, and scalable customer adoption.

1. Who can profit from teaming with us or purchasing the solution?

Manufacturers, government agencies, defense contractors, and research institutions can leverage Nimble to address digital engineering, physical AI, cybersecurity, compliance, and operational challenges. Partners (i.e KDM) can generate revenue through software licensing, implementation, integration, and related services.

2. How can RBC or Southern Additive Lab profit from applying the solution with manufacturers?

RBC and Southern Additive Lab can incorporate Nimble into their existing manufacturing relationships and service offerings, creating revenue opportunities through licensing, implementation, integration, and technical services. Southern University’s existing research and industry ecosystem also presents opportunities to extend adoption across manufacturing and applied research.

3. What opportunities can E3S bring to RBC to generate revenue through contract awards?

Southern University’s relationships with NASA and KDM’s relationships with the Department of Energy (DOE) are examples of existing ecosystems where we can explore opportunities to apply Nimble. Our partners can bring forward customer challenges and operational requirements, and we can jointly develop use cases and demonstrate how the CDT addresses those needs.

E3S and Nimble provide the software capabilities and customer enablement needed to validate use cases and develop scalable, repeatable solutions that can be deployed across similar environments. This creates opportunities to incorporate software licensing, implementation, and related services into contract awards while expanding adoption across government, research, and industry.

Revenue Sharing and Distribution

Software licensing support 10–15% license-margin sharing, contingent on transactions going through our TDS distribution channels. We are also working with distribution partners to identify strategic accounts that may qualify for enhanced margins of up to 40%, subject to deal registration and applicable requirements.

The revenue-sharing arrangements between KDM and E3S, and separately between RBC and SU, should reflect each party’s contributions to software licensing, customer enablement, and delivery. Eligibility for applicable distribution margins and enhanced margin opportunities is contingent on identifying and registering the deal through our TDS distribution channels and meeting the relevant program requirements.
