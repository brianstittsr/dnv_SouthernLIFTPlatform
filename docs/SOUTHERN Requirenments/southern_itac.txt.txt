Newly Funded Opportunity: 

University-Based Industrial Training and Assessment Centers (ITAC)



EnergyWerx and the Department of Energy's (DOE) Office of Critical Minerals and Energy Innovation (CMEI) have launched a newly funded Opportunity for U.S. colleges and universities: the University-Based Industrial Training and Assessment Centers (ITAC). 

 

The goal of the University-Based ITAC program is twofold: first, to help U.S. manufacturing competitiveness by providing assessments and recommendations to improve operations for small and medium-sized manufacturers/enterprises (SMEs), and second, to address a shortage of engineering professionals with applied energy and manufacturing-related skills by training engineering students through hands-on involvement in these assessments.  

 

University-Based Track 1 – Manufacturing ITACs 

Universities selected to operate Manufacturing ITACs are expected to continue providing technical assistance and assessments to eligible small and medium-sized manufacturers (SMEs) meeting defined size, sales, employee, and energy-use requirements. 

 

University-Based Track 2 – Mining and Mineral Processing ITACs

Universities selected to operate specialized ITACs will provide technical assistance to eligible SMEs in mining and mineral processing (SIC Codes 10–14, 32, and 33) with annual sales below $250M and fewer than 500 employees. 

 

How to Apply

All applications for both University-Based ITAC Track 1 and Track 2 are due October 30, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET. Applications are facilitated through EnergyWerx to assist organizations with varying levels of federal funding experience.
