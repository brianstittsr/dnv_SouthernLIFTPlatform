PURPOSE
-------
This catalog lists every question the platform asks today (Part A, taken
directly from the platform code) and every question the LIFT program will ask
across the full client lifecycle (Part B, from the LIFT Gap Analysis, LIFT
System Design, and the MBDA Client Intake & Letter of Engagement form). Use it
to review wording, remove duplicates, confirm required fields, and replace
KDM / SVP branding before LIFT launch.

  (Optional)      May be skipped.

  multi-select, checkbox (yes/no), list entry (add multiple values),
  file upload, signature, rating, NPS (0-10), Likert scale

            changed for the LIFT platform.
  NOTE      Behavior worth reviewing (validation gaps, outdated fields, etc.).

Question 2.
