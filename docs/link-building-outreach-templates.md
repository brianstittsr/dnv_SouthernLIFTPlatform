**Link Quality Metrics:**
- Domain Authority of linking sites
- Relevance to federal contracting/CMMC
- Local vs. national link distribution
- Government (.gov) and education (.edu) links
- Industry authority site links

**Outreach Performance:**
- Email open rates (target: 25%+)

- Referral traffic from new links
- Improvements in search rankings
- Domain authority growth
- Local search visibility improvements
- Brand mention increases

2. **Link Quality Assessment**: Evaluate new links for authority and relevance
3. **Competitor Monitoring**: Track competitor link acquisition
4. **Relationship Management**: Follow up on pending opportunities
5. **Strategy Adjustment**: Refine targeting based on results
