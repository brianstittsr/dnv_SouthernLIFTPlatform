## Slide 5: AI Teaming — Assemble Winning Teams Automatically
**Title:** Intelligent Matching for Complex Government Contracts

### Visual
- Network diagram showing AI matching members to contracts
- Icons: Brain, Team, Target, Trophy

### Key Points
**The Challenge:**
Large government contracts often require multiple capabilities — manufacturing, engineering, logistics, compliance — that no single small business has alone.

**The AI Teaming Solution:**
- 🎯 **Gap Analysis** — AI identifies what capabilities a contract needs that you don't have
- 🔍 **Smart Matching** — Finds consortium members who fill those exact gaps
- 📊 **Compatibility Scoring** — 0-100 score based on past performance, certs, geography, capacity
- 💡 **Team Structure Suggestions** — AI recommends Prime, Technical Lead, Manufacturing, Compliance roles

**How It Works:**
1. **Identify Opportunity** — You find or get matched to a contract
2. **Request Analysis** — AI analyzes required vs. available capabilities
3. **Get Recommendations** — Receive ranked list of potential teammates
4. **Contact & Team** — Reach out via in-platform messaging to form your team
5. **Submit Together** — Pursue contract as a consortium team

**Real Results:**
- ⚡ Form teams in hours, not weeks
- 🎯 Target contracts 2-5x larger than solo capacity
- 🤝 Build lasting partnerships with vetted members
- 📈 Increase win rate through stronger team positioning

### Speaker Notes
Here's where the magic really happens — AI Teaming. Government contracts are getting larger and more complex. A single contract might need manufacturing, engineering, cybersecurity, logistics, and compliance — capabilities that no one small business has completely. Traditionally, finding teammates takes weeks of networking and vetting. Our AI Teaming system does it in minutes. When you identify an opportunity, the AI analyzes what capabilities are required versus what you have. It then finds consortium members who fill those exact gaps and gives them a compatibility score based on past performance, certifications, geography, and availability. It even suggests a team structure — who should be prime, who handles technical, manufacturing, compliance. You reach out through the platform, form your team, and submit together. This lets you pursue contracts two to five times larger than you could solo, and you're teaming with members who are already vetted and aligned with the KDM mission.
