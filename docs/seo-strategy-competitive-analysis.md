# KDM & Associates SEO Strategy & Competitive Analysis

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive SEO strategy and competitive analysis for KDM & Associates focuses on positioning the company as a leading federal procurement consulting firm, with particular emphasis on CMMC (Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification) services. The analysis identifies key competitors, market gaps, and provides a detailed implementation plan to improve organic search visibility and competitive positioning.
