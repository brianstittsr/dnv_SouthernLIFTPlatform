## Government Contracting Fundamentals

### Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR)
- Primary regulation governing U.S. federal procurement
- FAR Part 19: Small Business Programs
- FAR Part 15: Contracting by Negotiation
- DFARS: Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DoD-specific)

### Small Business Certifications
| Certification | Agency | Key Benefit |
|--------------|--------|-------------|
| 8(a) Business Development | SBA | Sole-source contracts up to $4.5M ($7M for manufacturing) |
| WOSB (Women-Owned) | SBA | Set-aside and sole-source contracts |
| SDVOSB (Service-Disabled Veteran) | SBA/VA | VA set-aside priority |
| HUBZone | SBA | Price evaluation preference (10%) |
| MBE (Minority Business Enterprise) | NMSDC | Corporate supplier diversity access |
| DBE (Disadvantaged Business Enterprise) | DOT | Transportation contract set-asides |

### CMMC (Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification)
- **Level 1**: Basic cyber hygiene (17 practices, self-assessment)
- **Level 2**: Advanced cyber hygiene (110 practices, third-party assessment)
- **Level 3**: Expert (110+ practices, government-led assessment)
- **CUI**: Controlled Unclassified Information (requires Level 2+)
- **FCI**: Federal Contract Information (requires Level 1+)
- **Timeline**: Required for all DoD contracts by 2026

### Contract Vehicles
- **GSA Schedule**: Pre-negotiated government-wide contracts
- **GWAC**: Government-Wide Acquisition Contracts (IT-focused)
- **BPA**: Blanket Purchase Agreements
- **IDIQ**: Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity
- **SBIR/STTR**: Small Business Innovation Research

## Proof Pack Methodology

### Document Categories
1. **Capability Statement** - 1-2 page company summary for government buyers
2. **Past Performance** - Documented contract history with outcomes
3. **Certifications** - ISO, CMMC, small business certifications
4. **Financial Capability** - Bonding capacity, financial statements
5. **Technical Qualifications** - Relevant expertise documentation
6. **Safety/Compliance** - OSHA records, environmental compliance
7. **Quality Systems** - QMS documentation, procedures

### Pack Health Scoring Algorithm
```
Pack Health = Weighted sum of:
  - Document completeness (30%)
  - Document currency (20%) - not expired
  - Document quality (20%) - format, detail level
  - Certification validity (15%) - active certifications
  - Gap remediation progress (15%) - planned fixes underway
```

### Thresholds
- **0-30**: Critical gaps, immediate action needed
- **31-50**: Significant gaps, structured remediation plan
- **51-69**: Minor gaps, close to ready
- **70-85**: Intro-eligible, active buyer matching
- **86-100**: Premium status, priority introductions

## EOS/Traction System Knowledge

### Core Components
- **Vision/Traction Organizer (VTO)**: Company alignment document
- **Rocks**: 90-day priorities (3-7 per person)
- **Scorecard**: Weekly measurable metrics
- **Issues List**: IDS process (Identify, Discuss, Solve)
- **To-Dos**: 7-day action items
- **Level 10 Meetings**: Weekly 90-minute structured meetings

### Meeting Structure (L10)
1. Segue (5 min) - Good news, personal/professional
2. Scorecard Review (5 min) - Weekly numbers
3. Rock Review (5 min) - On/off track
4. Customer/Employee Headlines (5 min)
5. To-Do List (5 min) - Last week completion
6. IDS (60 min) - Solve issues in priority order
7. Conclude (5 min) - Recap, rate 1-10

### GWC Assessment
- **Gets It**: Understands the role and company culture
- **Wants It**: Genuinely desires the position
- **Capacity to Do It**: Has the skills, time, and emotional bandwidth

## Revenue Share Model Details

### Attribution Calculation
```
For each transaction:
  1. Who generated the lead? -> 20% attribution
  2. Who delivered the service? -> 50% attribution
  3. Who facilitated the introduction? -> 20% attribution
  4. Platform fee (always KDM) -> 10% attribution

Example: $10,000 contract engagement
  - V+ generated lead: $2,000 (20%)
  - LogiCore delivered service: $5,000 (50%)
  - ADA facilitated buyer intro: $2,000 (20%)
  - KDM Platform: $1,000 (10%)
```

### Settlement Cadence
- Quarterly settlement calculations
- 7-day hold period before payout
- Minimum payout threshold: configurable per partner
- Payout methods: Stripe Connect, bank transfer, manual

### Dispute Resolution
1. Partner raises dispute via platform
2. Admin reviews attribution chain
3. Audit log provides full transaction history
4. Resolution within 5 business days
5. Disputed amounts held until resolved
