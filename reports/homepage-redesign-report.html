1 · Executive Summary

The KDM platform has quietly become something most GovCon consultancies cannot offer: a working system — member registration, a guided Company Intelligence onboarding wizard, a SAM.gov opportunity pipeline with AI matching, AI-generated teaming recommendations, a member portal, events, press releases, and an admin back office. The current homepage tells almost none of that story. It leads with a generic consulting narrative and hardcoded performance numbers that cannot be substantiated from the platform itself.

The core recommendation: stop selling the consulting pitch and start showing the machine. The redesigned homepage should walk a visitor through a provable sequence — join → build your intelligence profile → get matched to real SAM.gov opportunities → get recommended a teaming partner → pursue as part of a Five-Pillar consortium. Every claim on the page should trace to data the system can show on demand.

Three moves

  1. Redesign the homepage around the real product. Reorder the page to lead with what KDM is (a consortium + a working platform), then the pipeline (SAM.gov leads → AI matching → teaming), then the network and Five Pillars, closing with momentum (new KDM Gallery) and proof (live metrics).
  2. Replace aspirational claims with provable ones. The "475 Clients / $50B+ / 14+ agreements / 30+ partners" strip and the "500+ directory" line cannot be verified by the system. Section 3 rewrites every claim into language that is either live-computed or defensibly phrased.
  3. Shift pricing from one-time sales to network-driven subscriptions. Today the only recurring product is the $625/mo Consortium membership; Founders ($625) and CMMC Cohort ($7,500) are one-time. Section 5 proposes a tier ladder plus subscription add-ons — buyer-side subscriptions, an alerts/matching SaaS tier, retainers, sponsorships — modeled on how GovWin, GovTribe, HigherGov, USFCR, and the associations actually make recurring money.
Honesty note on current numbers. As of this report the platform shows 31 team members, 23 consortium members, 32 portal users, 3 press releases, and 4 company profiles. The SAM.gov pipeline is built but has delivered 0 matched opportunities and 0 teaming recommendations so far (the eligibility funnel is being fixed in parallel work). This report treats that honestly: the live-metrics section is designed to switch on as real activity accrues, and Phase 1 of the roadmap requires no inflated numbers at all.

3 · Claims Audit — What We Can (and Can't) Prove

The platform already carries a careful government-affiliation disclaimer in the footer and a Readiness-Badge disclaimer on pricing — the right instinct. The homepage stats strip and a few marketing lines go the other way: hardcoded figures with no data source behind them. In GovCon marketing, unsupported performance claims carry real FTC truth-in-advertising and reputational risk — especially claims read as endorsement or past-performance.

3.1 Claim-by-claim review

Current claimVerdictIssueRecommended replacement
"475 Clients" Unverifiable No client registry supports it; the platform shows 31 team / 23 consortium / 32 portal members today. "Clients served across 15+ years of federal advisory work" — only if Keith can substantiate via engagement records; otherwise replace with live count: "{N} member companies" pulled from Firestore.
"$50B+ Contract Transactions" High risk Reads as awards won. If it represents cumulative team/firm experience across past employers, it isn't KDM's number to claim. Attribute honestly: "Advisory leadership with experience spanning billions in federal transactions" — and only if Keith approves the wording as accurate for KDM & Associates specifically. Otherwise remove; the pipeline metrics below are stronger anyway.
"14+ Shared Outcome Agreements" Substantiate Plausibly real but untracked in the system. Either keep with Keith's written confirmation of the count, or migrate the agreements into the platform and make it a live count. Recommend the latter — it becomes self-proving.
"30+ Resource Partners" Substantiate resourcePartners collection is currently empty. Populate the collection (or map to an existing partner list) and render the live count. Until then: "a growing resource-partner network."
"500+ directory-listed contractors, suppliers and government buyers" (pricing page) Unverifiable Platform membership is ~23–32 accounts; mock/directory seed data should never anchor a sales claim. "a curated network of member companies" + live member count. Never price-copy numbers the directory can't demonstrate.
"Clients who transformed their government contracting business" (testimonials intro) OK if real Fine only if every testimonial is from a real, consenting client — verify the testimonial records are genuine. Keep, but ensure testimonials carry name/company the member directory can corroborate.

4 · KDM Gallery — New Momentum Section

Per direction, the Gallery combines an automatic momentum feed (real network activity) with an admin-curated photo strip (events, handshakes, site visits). Positioned after the Five Pillars and before the metrics band — it answers the prospect's silent question: "is this network actually alive?"

4.1 Layout concept

KDM Gallery — "The network in motion" · newest members · latest events · recent press
Row A — Momentum feed (auto): card carousel of latest activity — "Acme Fabrication joined the Consortium · Manufacturing pillar", "IAEOZ Summit 2025 · Nov 12–14", "New press release: KDM announces…", "142 opportunities matched this month".
Row B — Photo strip (curated): 4–6 admin-selected photos with captions from the existing image library (event photos, teaming signings, site visits).

4.2 Data sources (all already exist)

Feed itemSourceNotes
Newest members (name, company, pillar)teamMembers / consortiumMembers, ordered by createdAtPublic directory already exposes this data — reuse the members-grid pattern.
Upcoming & recent eventsevents collection + events-data.tsAction: migrate the 6 static events into the Firestore events collection so the admin event-manager drives both the /events page and the Gallery.
Press releasespressReleases (3 today)Auto-card: title + publish date + link.
Match/teaming counterssamgovOpportunities, aiTeamingRecommendations, samgovSyncRunsRenders only when non-zero; e.g. "AI matched 212 opportunities in the last 30 days."
Curated photosExisting ImageManager library (Firebase Storage)New "gallery" tag/flag on stored images; admin picks captions + publish toggle in the existing hero admin or a small Gallery admin card.

4.3 Content rules

  • Auto items are real by construction — they render only if the underlying record exists, so nothing can overstate activity.
  • Privacy: show member company names/logos only (same data already public in the directory); never emails, match scores, or member-specific opportunity titles.
  • Empty state: if no feed items exist yet, the section collapses to the curated photo strip only — never shows "0 new members."
  • Cadence: the photo strip depends on Keith's team photographing events — recommend a standing "3 photos per event" habit; this is the one operational dependency.

5 · Recurring-Revenue Product & Pricing Strategy

Current pricing: KDM Consortium $625/mo (or $6,750/yr) · Founders $625 one-time · CMMC Cohort $7,500 one-time. Two of three revenue products are one-time — exactly the hunt-forever model to retire. The market reference points (Section 6) show the standard GovCon playbook: a free/cheap network layer feeding paid tiers, plus subscription add-ons that compound as network activity grows.

5.1 Recommended membership ladder

TierSuggested priceWhat it unlocksRole in the funnel
Network (free)Free Directory listing, public events, monthly market-intel digest, KDM Gallery presence Feeds the top of funnel; every free member makes the paid matching pool bigger (network effect).
Consortium Member~$495–625/mo (anchor at current $625) SAM.gov opportunity matching, portal, teaming recommendations, member-only events, proposal-resource library The core product — keep current price; the new homepage finally justifies it.
Alliance / Prime tier~$1,500–2,500/mo Everything in Member + priority pursuit notifications, dedicated capture support hours, featured directory placement, co-marketing, direct introductions to buyers For companies actively pursuing — high-margin, sells services capacity KDM already provides ad hoc.

Price points are directional for Keith's review — validated against GovTribe ($1,350–5,500/yr for software alone), GovWin ($12k–42k/yr), and USFCR ($599–5k+ for registration services). KDM's membership bundles software + humans, which supports premium pricing.

Every subscription product above monetizes network activity — matches made, partners found, buyers connected — rather than Keith's personal hours. That is the structural fix for the "always hunting new clients" problem: the network itself becomes the product being renewed.

6.5 Offerings worth adopting — shortlist

  1. Registration/certification concierge (USFCR model) — monetize the onboarding wizard; pairs with the free APEX channel rather than competing with it.
  2. Standalone alerts/matching SaaS tier (GovTribe/HigherGov model) — sell the pipeline unbundled.
  3. Buyer-side subscription (GovWin teaming-network model) — the missing two-sided-market revenue; primes pay to access vetted suppliers.
  4. Managed capture / proposal retainer (OST Catalyst model) — services revenue that renews monthly.
  5. Verification badge subscription (USFCR seal model) — cheap, high-margin, and it strengthens directory trust.
  6. Company-size-scaled dues (SAME model) — optional alternative to flat pricing if Keith wants lower entry friction.
  7. Event sponsorships + training pass (association model) — turns Gallery activity into revenue.
What to avoid: competing on raw opportunity-feed volume (crowded, commoditized), enterprise sales-led pricing (no sales team), and any "win-rate" or "contracts won" marketing — the big firms get away with soft claims that would be riskier for a newer brand.
